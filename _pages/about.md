Hello! I am Albert, a PhD student at [California Institute of Technology](https://www.caltech.edu) in the Computing and Mathematical Sciences (CMS) department, advised by Professor [Katherine Bouman](https://users.cms.caltech.edu/~klbouman/). I have broad research interests in computational imaging, diffusion models, and inverse problems. I am supported by the [Kortschak Scholar Program](https://www.cms.caltech.edu/research/kortschak-scholars).

Previously, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics from [Washington University in St. Louis](https://washu.edu) (WashU) summa cum laude, where I was fortunate to be working on machine learning problems in [Professor Kamilov's](https://ukmlv.github.io) [Computational Imaging Group](https://cigroup.wustl.edu) and [Professor Vorobeychik's](https://vorobeychik.com) CERL Lab.
